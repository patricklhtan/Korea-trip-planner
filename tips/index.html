Travel Tips

Small things that smooth out a Seoul trip — payment, navigation, etiquette.

credit_card

Get a T-money card

Buy at any 7-Eleven or CU (~₩3,000 deposit). Tap on subway, buses, even taxis. Top up at any convenience store.

wifi

Rent a pocket WiFi or buy a SIM

Order at Klook for pickup at Incheon. KT/SK both reliable. Korean apps need a Korean number for SMS sometimes — pocket WiFi avoids that.

map

Use KakaoMap or Naver Map

Google Maps walking directions don't work well in Korea. KakaoMap or Naver Map is what locals use — both have English UI.

currency_exchange

Cash + cards

Most places take card, but small markets (Namdaemun, Gwangjang) and some traditional restaurants are cash-only. Get ₩100–200k cash on arrival.

translate

Papago > Google Translate

Naver's Papago handles Korean nuance much better than Google. Has a camera-translate mode for menus and signs.

thermostat

Late May weather

Late spring is warm (18–25 °C) with occasional rain. Bring layers and a compact umbrella.

subway

Subway etiquette

Priority seats stay empty for elderly/pregnant even on empty trains. Keep voices low. Don't eat on the train.

restaurant

Dining basics

No tipping. Side dishes (banchan) are free and refillable. Cheers with both hands on the cup for elders.

shopping_bag

Tax refund

Stores with 'Tax Free' sticker refund VAT for purchases over ₩30,000. Get the form, refund at ICN before departure.

directions_bus

Airport bus 6702

Stops right outside Seoul Dragon City. Buy ticket at the bus stop counter (~₩17,000). Runs every 20–30 min.
